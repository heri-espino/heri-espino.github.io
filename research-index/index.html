Manuscripts in review or revision

  • Unit-Region Factorization for Empty-Region Proximity Graphs — Under Review; Elsevier · Discrete Applied Mathematics · Special Issue LAGOS 2025; Heriberto Espino Montelongo and Héctor Maravillo. A fixed-template empty-region rule assigns a Borel region (S(p,q)) to a candidate pair and retains the edge when that region contains no other site. The manuscript isolates the unit-volume constant (a_K) for similarity-copy regions and shows how the same scalar controls exact Poisson void probabilities, incident-edge length intensities, mean out-degree, and normalized incident-edge length laws.

  • Geometry and Volume of Stepping-Stone Diversion Neighborhoods in Euclidean Spaces of Arbitrary Dimension — Under Revision; Elsevier · Computational Geometry; Heriberto Espino Montelongo and Héctor Maravillo. This manuscript develops a geometric, graph-theoretic, and probabilistic analysis of the stepping-stone graph in (\mathbb{R}^d) for every (d\geq 2). It derives a normalized one-dimensional volume integral, recovers the degenerate and Gabriel cases and the relative-neighborhood limit, proves strict region and volume monotonicity, establishes graph inclusions and connectivity, and validates a stable quadrature formula by rejection sampling.

  • When Do Activation-Patching Effects Add Up? Evidence from Controlled CNNs — Under Review; Springer Nature · SN Computer Science; Heriberto Espino-Montelongo. This study asks when the effects of joint activation-channel patches can be predicted by summing individual channel changes. In controlled convolutional networks, intervention-size effects can occur even under exact logit additivity; other architectures exhibit substantial departures that can reverse a training-regime contrast.

  • Template Priors in Small CNNs: Learning Dynamics and the Measurement Dependence of Causal Usefulness — Under Review; Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR) · Double-blind review; Heriberto Espino Montelongo. A controlled study of template initialization, retention, and release in small CNNs on two rendering tasks. Across staged experiments with (n=400) primary runs, (n=200) gradual-release runs, (80) final checkpoints, and (2{,}000) saved states, template initialization does not uniformly accelerate learning; releasing retention improves compositional accuracy relative to constant retention while reducing alignment; and the relative causal-usefulness difference changes with channel ranking and intervention size.

Other manuscripts, working drafts, lectures, and tutorials

  • Smoothness-Integrated Trend Distances with Nonzero Difference Drift — Working Draft (Working draft); Research note · Not submitted. This working research note compares complete smoothness-indexed families of penalized least-squares (PLS) trends in a Hilbert-space framework. It permits nonzero reference drift in finite-difference penalties, distinguishes that drift from the signed mean gap between fitted trends, and derives global, rolling-window, and smoothness–time distances with spectral representations for shared and unequal drifts.

  • Dynamic Forecast-Optimal Smoothness for Penalized Trend Estimation — Working Draft (Research manuscript draft); Manuscript draft · Submission not verified. Develops a framework for tracking multiple local minima of chronological forecast-loss surfaces across forecast origins. Persistent branches retain smoothness, first-stage validation loss, and refitted subsequent loss; branch-selection and branch-to-smoothness rules are evaluated separately. Results favor horizon-matched pooled forecasting in controlled simulations, while broader tests do not establish a universally superior dynamic rule.

  • Smoothness-Indexed Penalized Trend Estimation in Python: Parameter-Search Coordinates, Numerical Backends, and Reproducible Comparisons — Working Draft (Research manuscript draft); Methods/software manuscript · Submission not verified. Presents a reproducible Python implementation of Guerrero-style penalized least-squares smoothing with an order-normalized smoothness coordinate. Distinguishes parameter-search coordinate from statistical criterion and compares seven numerical backends in an archived 80,640-record factorial study. Independent paired timing experiments and four root-finding methods examine accuracy and performance, with CO₂ and federal funds examples.

  • Posterior Integration and Volatility Inputs in WTI Asian Options — Working Draft (Blinded manuscript draft); Blinded research manuscript · Submission not verified. Studies whether Bayesian posterior integration changes WTI average-price option values as much as the volatility information supplied to the pricing model. A 10-million-history synthetic experiment separates curvature and posterior-dispersion effects. The original date-ordered sample and a separate settlement-mark benchmark show much lower pricing errors using earlier option-implied volatility than historical-volatility posterior integration under their respective modeling assumptions.

  • Componentwise kNN-Isomap: From Neighborhood Graphs to Constrained Manifold Embeddings — Research Lecture (Mathematical lecture); 53-slide mathematical lecture · Not a journal submission. A mathematical lecture deriving the graph-shortest-path, classical MDS, and rigid-placement stages of componentwise Isomap. It distinguishes connected-component discovery from independent embedding, states a constructive strip-separation guarantee, and uses completed EXP-010/EXP-011 experiments to illustrate distortion and separation trade-offs. A more ambitious separation-constrained optimization is explicitly proposed, not implemented.

  • From Max-Cut to Semidefinite Programming, EVA, and Clustering-Based Rounding — Research Tutorial (Mathematical tutorial); 7-page tutorial · Not a journal submission. A step-by-step mathematical tutorial on weighted Max-Cut, sign and Laplacian formulations, semidefinite relaxation, low-rank unit-vector factorization and Efficient Vector Ascent (EVA). It compares Goemans–Williamson random-hyperplane rounding with two spherical clustering choices, K-Means2N and K-MeansNM, and explains saved-factor reuse, independent rounding restarts, and G-set evaluation.

  • Beyond the Incentive Threshold: Academic Support Use and Persistence After a First-Year Participation Incentive — Working Paper (Working paper); Higher education · 2026. A longitudinal study of (n=3{,}241) students examining whether formal mathematics-support use persists from an incentive-linked first-year context into later Calculus I use after that same incentive context is no longer assumed to apply.

  • Mathematics Support Use and Classroom-Relative Performance in First-Year University Mathematics — Research Draft (Research draft); Internal development · Sep 2026. An observational study of (n=6{,}627) first-year mathematics students linking same-period mathematics-support use to classroom-relative academic performance while keeping self-selection and non-causal interpretation explicit.

  • Are Course Grades Comparable Across Classrooms? Instructor-by-Term Heterogeneity in Undergraduate Mathematics Assessment — Research Draft (Research draft); Internal development · Sep 2026. A measurement study of classroom-level grade heterogeneity in undergraduate mathematics, asking whether a common numerical grade (g) can be treated as empirically exchangeable across instructor-by-term contexts.

  • From Assigned to Chosen Academic Contexts: Longitudinal Traces of Student Engagement and Adaptation in First-Year University Mathematics — Research Draft (Research draft); Internal development · Sep 2026. An administrative-data study of (n=6{,}627) students that distinguishes academic context, experienced performance, formal help-seeking, later context selection, and subsequent outcomes. The analysis identifies five first-course states, examines later Calculus enrolment, and follows adaptation after failed first-course attempts while keeping the findings descriptive rather than causal.

  • Bayesian Parameter Uncertainty in WTI Average Price Option Valuation — Working Paper (Working paper); Internal working draft · 2026. A working study of Bayesian parameter uncertainty in WTI average-price option valuation. It separates statistical learning under the physical measure from no-arbitrage valuation under the risk-neutral measure and propagates posterior uncertainty in volatility into arithmetic-average option prices. The empirical design combines (213) unique call and put contracts with (11{,}179) option-date observations spanning September 2026 to June 2029.

  • A Reproducible Comparison of Trend Estimation Methods on S&P 500 Log Prices — Software Project (Software project); Reproducible empirical comparison. Empirical comparison of train-validation smoothness selection for penalized trends (\widehat{t}(\lambda)) against time-weighted validation and benchmark methods on S&P 500 log prices. The design uses a strict temporal train/validation/test protocol and separates Guerrero smoothness from realized roughness diagnostics.

The bivariate theft–homicide association is reported as (I=0.11) with (p=0.205), while local results distinguish HH, LH, LL, and non-significant areas.

  • Right-Censored Regression for IBD with CatBoost and XGBoost Stacking — academic-projects; Academic Project. A censor-aware stacking pipeline for an IBD target right-censored at (c=20). CatBoost is trained on uncensored cases using (K=9)-fold out-of-fold predictions, a robust residual scale is estimated, and Tobit-style tail corrections are applied before XGBoost meta-learning.

  • Proximity Graphs — presentations; Poster. A poster presenting a Python API for constructing, visualizing, and comparing proximity-graph families and biologically inspired models. It connects an empty-region area (A(\ell)=w\ell^2) with Poisson edge-survival probabilities and asymptotic graph properties.

  • Proximity Graphs: Construction and Asymptotic Characteristics — presentations; Poster. A poster on proximity-graph construction and asymptotic characteristics, including explicit area formulas for the β-lune and stepping-stone diversion kernels. For a Poisson process, it presents the empty-neighbourhood relation (\Pr(\mathrm{empty})=e^{-\rho w\ell^2}) and its connection to asymptotic expected degree.
