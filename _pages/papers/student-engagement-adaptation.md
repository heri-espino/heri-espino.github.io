The analysis identifies five first-course states, examines later Calculus enrolment, and follows adaptation after failed first-course attempts while keeping the findings descriptive rather than causal.'

contributions:
  - Distinguishes classroom context, classroom-relative performance, formal mathematics-support use, later context selection, and subsequent outcomes.
  - Summarizes first-course experiences into five states and tests their stability across nine pre-specified definitions of performance and contextual difficulty.
  - Examines later Calculus enrolment using instructor outcomes ranked from strictly prior information.
  - Shows how instructor changes and movement toward historically higher-outcome instructors appear after failed first-course attempts, without identifying causal effects or latent engagement.
