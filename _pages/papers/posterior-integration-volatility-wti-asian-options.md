**Related but distinct:** This is not a duplicate of the older *Bayesian Parameter Uncertainty in WTI Average Price Option Valuation* entry. The present draft focuses specifically on posterior integration versus the source of volatility information, with expanded empirical comparisons.

**Limitation:** The reported error differences are conditional on the chosen pricing approximations and observed market information, not unconditional trading-performance gains.
