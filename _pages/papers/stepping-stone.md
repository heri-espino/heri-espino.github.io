contributions:
  - 'Defines the normalized stepping-stone region \(K_{\mathrm{SS},\alpha}\) and factors its volume into pair distance and a normalized constant.'
  - 'Derives an explicit boundary parametrization and a one-dimensional integral formula for every \(d\geq 2\) and \(\alpha\geq 1\).'
  - Recovers the degenerate and Gabriel cases and proves convergence to the open relative-neighborhood lune.
  - Establishes strict region and volume monotonicity, graph inclusions, and connectivity in arbitrary dimension.
  - Gives a stable quadrature formula and validates it independently by rejection sampling.
