The [interactive author preview](https://heri-espino.github.io/proximitygraphs-siam-figures/article.html) accompanies the original LaTeX manuscript **The Empty Space That Makes a Network**, coauthored by **Heriberto Espino-Montelongo** and **Héctor Maravillo**. The work is intended as a possible *SIAM News — Software and Programming* feature, but **has not been submitted to, accepted by, published in, or endorsed by SIAM News**.

The narrative develops from John Snow's **road-distance** boundary for the 1855 parish inquiry to a separate modern **Euclidean** Voronoi reconstruction, then to empty-region graph families, scale-dependent boundaries, and a controlled comparison of Gabriel and relative-neighborhood graphs. Interactive figures let readers inspect the effects of moving sites, varying shape scale and changing neighborhood rules on fixed point locations.
