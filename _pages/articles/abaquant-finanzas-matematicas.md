The article was written with **Owen Paredes Conde** and published by **La Catarina**, a UDLAP publication, in its **Ciencias / IMEF** section. It explains the academic motivation for AbaQuant and surveys the library's principal financial and actuarial applications.

This is a **published technical article**, distinct from a peer-reviewed research manuscript. The publisher hosts the authoritative text: [read the original article](https://lacatarina.udlap.mx/2026/10/abaquant-finanzas-matematicas-valuacion-y-gestion-del-riesgo/).
